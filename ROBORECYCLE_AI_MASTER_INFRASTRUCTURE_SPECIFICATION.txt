1. PLATFORM DEFINITION
ROBORECYCLE.ai is an industrial technology recovery, identification, evaluation, testing, refurbishment, and recirculation platform.

ROBORECYCLE.ai establishes a structured industrial recovery infrastructure for identifying, acquiring, recovering, inspecting, testing, grading, documenting, refurbishing, and redistributing industrial electronics, electrical equipment, mechanical assemblies, robotic components, and industrial automation equipment.

The platform recovers qualifying equipment from:

Manufacturing facilities.
Industrial production lines.
Industrial automation systems.
Robotic installations.
Retired robotic systems.
Decommissioned machinery.
Factory modernization projects.
Industrial equipment replacement programs.
Surplus industrial inventories.
Industrial asset recovery operations.
The objective is to preserve the functional, technical, and economic value of industrial equipment beyond the operational lifecycle of its original installation.

ROBORECYCLE.ai creates an organized secondary industrial supply infrastructure through physical equipment recovery, technical evaluation, documented validation, digital identification, condition classification, and commercial inventory qualification.

ROBORECYCLE.ai operates within the PITN.ai ecosystem and integrates with ROBOPARTS.ai for product intelligence, inventory presentation, sourcing, and commercial distribution.

24. DEFINITIVE OPERATING WORKFLOW
ROBORECYCLE.ai operates through the following sequential workflow:

SOURCE Acquire or accept industrial equipment and retired machinery.

REGISTER Create acquisition and recovery project records.

IDENTIFY Establish manufacturer, model, part number, revision, and physical asset identity.

RECOVER Extract qualifying equipment and assemblies.

INSPECT Document physical condition and visible defects.

TEST Perform appropriate electrical and functional evaluations.

GRADE Assign documented condition and verification classifications.

REPAIR Evaluate and perform appropriate repairs and refurbishment.

DOCUMENT Create and maintain individual digital equipment passports.

QUALIFY Determine appropriate commercial or material disposition.

INVENTORY Register qualifying equipment in controlled inventory.

DISTRIBUTE Transfer qualifying commercial inventory to ROBOPARTS.ai.

RECIRCULATE Return suitable equipment to industrial maintenance, repair, replacement, and production applications.

TRACE Preserve subsequent lifecycle events and recorded equipment history.
