PLATFORM DEFINITIONS
ROBORECYCLE.ai

INDUSTRIAL EQUIPMENT RECOVERY, IDENTIFICATION, TESTING & REUSE

ROBORECYCLE.ai identifies, acquires, recovers, inspects, tests, grades, refurbishes, and redistributes valuable industrial electronics, electrical equipment, mechanical assemblies, and robotic equipment recovered from industrial automation systems, manufacturing facilities, production equipment, robotic installations, and retired industrial machinery.

The recovery operation focuses on industrial equipment retaining functional, repairable, replacement, or material value, including equipment that is obsolete, discontinued, difficult to source, expensive to replace, or subject to extended procurement lead times.

ROBORECYCLE.ai establishes a structured secondary industrial supply process through physical equipment recovery, technical evaluation, documented testing, digital identification, condition classification, and commercial redistribution.

ROBOPARTS.ai

INDUSTRIAL ROBOTICS & AUTOMATION PARTS DISCOVERY, SOURCING & DISTRIBUTION

ROBOPARTS.ai provides the commercial marketplace for industrial robotics, automation equipment, electronic assemblies, mechanical equipment, and industrial replacement products.

The platform supports product discovery, inventory presentation, product sourcing, buyer and seller connections, and commercial distribution.

INSPECTION, TESTING AND CONDITION GRADING

Evaluation includes:

Visual inspection.
Physical condition assessment.
Electrical testing.
Functional testing.
Model-specific diagnostics, where available.
Damage and deterioration assessment.
Repairability evaluation.
Technical documentation review.
Each item receives a condition classification supported by available inspection and testing records.

Equipment records distinguish between:

Inspected equipment.
Tested functional equipment.
Untested equipment.
Equipment requiring repair.
Refurbished equipment.
Equipment designated for further parts recovery.
Equipment designated for material recycling.
Functional condition and compatibility are not assumed without appropriate verification.

INTEGRATED ROBORECYCLE.ai + ROBOPARTS.ai OPERATING ARCHITECTURE
The two platforms operate through a connected industrial equipment recovery and distribution process.

STAGE 1 — SOURCE

Acquire retired industrial equipment, machinery, robotic systems, and automation assets.

STAGE 2 — IDENTIFY

Identify equipment, manufacturers, product descriptions, models, and available technical information.

STAGE 3 — RECOVER

Disassemble qualifying equipment and recover reusable industrial technology.

STAGE 4 — TEST

Perform appropriate visual, electrical, and functional evaluations.

STAGE 5 — GRADE

Document physical condition, technical results, repair requirements, and condition classification.

STAGE 6 — CATALOG

Create digital equipment records containing identification, provenance, condition, and testing information.

STAGE 7 — DISTRIBUTE

Enter qualifying inventory into ROBOPARTS.ai for commercial presentation and distribution.

STAGE 8 — RECIRCULATE

Return qualifying industrial equipment to maintenance, repair, replacement, and production applications.
