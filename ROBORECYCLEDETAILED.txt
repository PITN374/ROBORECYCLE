1. EXECUTIVE DEFINITION
ROBORECYCLE.ai provides robotics manufacturers with a structured industrial technology recovery and recirculation service.

The service enables robotics manufacturers to identify, recover, evaluate, document, refurbish, and commercialize qualifying industrial equipment and robotic components removed from retired, replaced, upgraded, returned, surplus, or decommissioned robotic systems.

ROBORECYCLE.ai establishes an organized process for recovering residual functional, technical, and economic value from industrial technology that might otherwise be discarded, liquidated without technical evaluation, or transferred into conventional recycling channels.

The platform provides manufacturers with a structured pathway to:

Identify recoverable industrial equipment.
Evaluate potential recovery opportunities.
Recover qualifying robotic equipment and assemblies.
Preserve manufacturer and product identification.
Inspect and test individual recovered components.
Establish documented equipment condition.
Repair and refurbish suitable equipment.
Create traceable digital equipment records.
Establish commercially qualified secondary inventory.
Redistribute qualifying equipment through ROBOPARTS.ai.
Recover residual equipment value.
Improve industrial equipment lifecycle visibility.
ROBORECYCLE.ai allows manufacturers to establish a more comprehensive equipment lifecycle strategy extending beyond the original manufacture, sale, installation, and operation of their robotic products.

Robotics manufacturers develop and supply sophisticated industrial systems containing valuable electronic, electrical, mechanical, and robotic assemblies.

These systems may contain:

Robot controllers.
Servo drives.

Robotic joints.

Machine vision systems.
Encoders.

Control boards.
Input/output modules.
Communication equipment.
Teach pendants.
Electrical harnesses.
Industrial interfaces.
Specialized electronic assemblies.
When a robotic installation is retired, upgraded, replaced, or decommissioned, the remaining equipment does not necessarily lose its functional or commercial value.

Individual components may remain:

Functional.
Repairable.
Refurbishable.
Suitable for replacement inventory.
Useful for manufacturer service operations.
Valuable as discontinued replacement equipment.

ROBORECYCLE.ai addresses this operational gap.

It provides a defined recovery process that separates equipment with continued productive potential from equipment requiring further repair, parts recovery, material recovery, or recycling.

3. THE MANUFACTURER SERVICES MODEL
ROBORECYCLE.ai provides manufacturers with six principal service categories.

SERVICE A — INDUSTRIAL ASSET RECOVERY

Identification, acquisition, collection, and physical recovery of qualifying industrial equipment.

SERVICE B — EQUIPMENT IDENTIFICATION AND CATALOGING

Manufacturer, model, part number, revision, serial number, and product identity documentation.

SERVICE C — TECHNICAL INSPECTION AND VALIDATION

Physical inspection, electrical testing, functional testing, condition grading, and technical documentation.

SERVICE D — REPAIR AND REFURBISHMENT

Evaluation and restoration of equipment suitable for repair, refurbishment, or subsequent commercial use.

SERVICE E — DIGITAL EQUIPMENT PASSPORTS AND TRACEABILITY

Individual equipment identities, provenance, inspection records, testing histories, repair records, and lifecycle documentation.

SERVICE F — SECONDARY INVENTORY AND COMMERCIAL DISTRIBUTION

Qualification and commercial redistribution of suitable recovered equipment through ROBOPARTS.ai.

These services can be engaged individually or combined into an integrated manufacturer recovery program.

9. MANUFACTURER COMMERCIAL ENGAGEMENT MODELS
ROBORECYCLE.ai supports multiple commercial arrangements.

MODEL A — DIRECT EQUIPMENT ACQUISITION

ROBORECYCLE.ai purchases qualifying equipment from the manufacturer.

The manufacturer receives agreed acquisition proceeds.

ROBORECYCLE.ai assumes the agreed commercial responsibilities and inventory risk.

MODEL B — CONTRACTED RECOVERY SERVICES

The manufacturer contracts ROBORECYCLE.ai to identify, recover, inspect, document, and process specified equipment.

The manufacturer pays agreed service fees.

Equipment ownership and subsequent disposition remain subject to the contract.

MODEL C — CONSIGNMENT

The manufacturer retains ownership of designated equipment.

ROBORECYCLE.ai manages agreed recovery, processing, inventory preparation, and commercial distribution activities.

Sale proceeds are distributed according to the consignment agreement.

MODEL D — REVENUE SHARING

The manufacturer and ROBORECYCLE.ai agree to share proceeds from qualifying equipment sales.

The agreement defines recovery costs, processing costs, distribution expenses, and the applicable revenue-sharing calculation.

MODEL E — MANUFACTURER RETENTION AND INTERNAL REUSE

Recovered equipment is processed and documented for the manufacturer's own internal service inventory, refurbishment operation, training requirements, or replacement programs.

MODEL F — HYBRID RECOVERY PROGRAM

The manufacturer combines direct acquisition, contracted services, internal retention, consignment, and commercial redistribution.

Each equipment category receives the disposition most appropriate to its condition, ownership, commercial value, and intended use.

12. MANUFACTURER PROGRAM IMPLEMENTATION
A manufacturer can establish a ROBORECYCLE.ai program through six implementation phases.

PHASE 1 — INITIAL CONSULTATION

Identify the manufacturer's equipment retirement, surplus inventory, replacement, and recovery requirements.

PHASE 2 — EQUIPMENT ASSESSMENT

Review equipment lists, product categories, available documentation, photographs, locations, and preliminary recovery opportunities.

PHASE 3 — COMMERCIAL AGREEMENT

Establish ownership, authorization, scope, responsibilities, pricing, transportation, testing, disposition, and commercial terms.

PHASE 4 — RECOVERY EXECUTION

Coordinate equipment collection, disassembly, identification, physical recovery, and inventory registration.

PHASE 5 — TECHNICAL PROCESSING

Perform inspection, testing, grading, repair assessment, refurbishment, documentation, and commercial qualification.

PHASE 6 — DISTRIBUTION AND REPORTING

Transfer qualifying inventory to ROBOPARTS.ai, complete agreed commercial activities, and provide manufacturer recovery reports.

The program may begin with one equipment category, one facility, one retired robotic installation, or one surplus inventory group.

Successful pilot programs may subsequently expand into recurring manufacturer recovery agreements.

15. MANUFACTURER ACCOUNTABILITY AND QUALITY PRINCIPLES
ROBORECYCLE.ai establishes transparent distinctions between:

Manufacturer-provided specifications.
Original equipment records.
AI-assisted identification.
Technician-confirmed identification.
Physical inspection results.
Electrical testing results.
Functional testing results.
Repair records.
Refurbishment records.
Compatibility research.
Independently verified information.
Recovered equipment is not automatically equivalent to new equipment.

Functional testing does not automatically establish compatibility with another robotic system.

Recovered equipment is not automatically manufacturer-authorized, manufacturer-certified, or approved for a particular installation.

All applicable ownership, intellectual property, licensing, safety, warranty, export-control, and manufacturer authorization requirements must be respected.

ROBORECYCLE.ai provides documented recovery and evaluation information.

It does not replace the manufacturer's responsibility for its own product certifications, engineering approvals, or safety-critical decisions.
